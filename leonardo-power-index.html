The studio

Small studio. Big infrastructure.

We've been building AI voice for four years — early enough to have made every mistake once, and long enough to know what holds up in front of a real customer at eleven at night.

In that time the same conversation kept happening. An owner hears "AI" and nods politely, because nobody has ever told them what it would actually do on a wet Tuesday afternoon. Ask the same person about their website and they're precise: what it cost, what it's for, and exactly what it isn't doing.

Questions

The things people ask us first.

Will the voice sound like a robot?
No. It uses the same natural-voice technology behind the assistants you already talk to, tuned to your tone — friendly, brisk, formal, whatever fits. You hear it and approve it before launch, and you can change what it says at any time.
What happens when it doesn't know the answer?
It says so, takes a message and hands over to you — by text, by email, or as a live transfer during opening hours. It never invents a price or makes a promise you haven't approved. Every hand-over lands in the CRM with the full transcript.
Do I need to change my phone system?
Usually not. Caller recognition works by matching the number that rings you against your CRM. For live transfers we can use your existing line or give you a new number that forwards to it.
Where does my customers' data live?
In a CRM you own, with a data-processing agreement in place from day one, and you can export the lot whenever you like. The platform runs on US infrastructure — the same footing as Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 and most software your business already uses — and the transfer is covered by the UK's approved safeguards. If your business has to keep data inside the UK or the EU, tell us before we build, because it changes what we can use. We set the rest up with GDPR in mind: consent, retention and deletion included.
How much does it cost?
A fixed quote for the design and build, then a monthly care plan that covers the voice, the CRM, hosting, monitoring and ongoing SEO and AEO work. You get both numbers after a 30-minute call — no surprises later.
How long does it take?
You see the design in the first week. Most sites are live within four to six weeks, with the voice and CRM trained and connected.
I already have a website. Can you just add the clever bits?
Often, yes. If the existing site is sound we can add the assistant, the CRM and the AEO layer to it. If it isn't, we'll tell you plainly and quote for a rebuild.
